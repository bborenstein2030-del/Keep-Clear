Privacy Policy

Last updated September 18, 2026

Keepclear is a planner that builds your day around your classes, tasks and sleep. This page explains what information Keepclear handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your plan is used only to run Keepclear for you. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

Using Keepclear without an account

You can use Keepclear without signing in. Everything you enter (events, tasks, goals, settings) is saved only in your own browser’s storage on your device. It is not sent to Keepclear’s servers.

Information we collect when you sign in

If you choose to create an account, Keepclear stores:

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the basic profile information that provider shares, such as your name and profile picture. We only ask Google for your name, email address and profile picture, and we never receive your Google or Apple password.
  • Your plan: the events, tasks, goals, habits, ideas, settings and history you add in Keepclear, so they can be saved and synced across your devices.
  • Technical sign-in data: a session token kept in your browser so you stay signed in, plus standard records our sign-in provider keeps for security (such as when you last signed in).

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To save your plan and show it on any device where you sign in.
  • To build your schedule. All planning happens inside the app from the information you enter.

We do not sell your information, show ads, or use your data to build advertising profiles. Keepclear does not use analytics or tracking cookies.

Files and calendars you import

When you import a calendar file (.ics) or a PDF syllabus, the file is read inside your browser. Only the events and tasks you choose to add become part of your plan.

Services Keepclear relies on

These companies process data only as needed to run Keepclear:

  • Supabase provides sign-in and stores account data and saved plans. Each account can only read its own data.
  • Google and Apple handle sign-in if you choose those options.
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like any web host, receives basic request information such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs deliver the fonts and code libraries the page uses. Your browser contacts them when loading the page, which shares your IP address with them.

How long we keep it

Your account and saved plan are kept until you delete them. Signing out removes your plan from that browser but keeps it in your account.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in Keepclear, go to Settings, then Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved in it.
  • Clear your data: Settings, then Clear all data, erases your plan while keeping your account.
  • Disconnect Google: you can remove Keepclear’s access at any time in your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules make sure a signed-in person can read and change only their own data. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Keepclear is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/bborenstein2030-del/Keep-Clear/issues.
